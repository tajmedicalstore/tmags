How to order

1. Browse Products and find the item you need.
2. Tap "🛒 Order" on the product, or "Ask" if you have a question first.
3. Fill in your details and submit.
4. We'll call to confirm — it depends how the shop currently works.

Good to know

Prices shown on this site are a guide — always confirm the final price and availability with us before paying, as medicine prices can change. For anything urgent, calling is fastest. We're open —, Monday to Sunday.
